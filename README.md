# Dad's Birthday Site 🍎📚

Open `index.html` (double-click). Tap through the pages, no scrolling.

## Pages, in order
1. **Happy Birthday Dad**: big animated title (tap anywhere to begin, this also starts the music)
2. **Your quote**: appears word by word on the chalkboard
3. **Photo stack**: tap a photo to flick to the next one (5 photos)
4. **Report card**: grades get stamped one by one
5. **The letter**: types itself out on notebook paper (tap once to show it all, again for next)
6. **Bake the cake**: tap the oven, wait for DING, then tap the cake to blow out the candles
7. **Hip hip hooray!**: confetti and balloons
8. **Heart tree**: "I love you, Dad" with a tree full of heart leaves (tap the tree for hearts)

## Your 5 photos
Put them in the `images/` folder named `photo1`, `photo2`, `photo3`, `photo4`, `photo5`
(.jpg, .jpeg, .png or .webp all work). Until then you will see a "add photo" placeholder.
Change the captions in `script.js` (section `CONTENT` > `photos`).

## Your song
Put an mp3 you have the rights to use at `audio/theme-song.mp3`.
It starts on the first tap. The round gold button (bottom right) is play/pause.

## Change any words
Everything is at the top of `script.js` in `CONTENT`: the quote, the letter, the
report-card grades and remark, photo captions.

## Put it online (GitHub Pages)
New Public repository, upload everything (including the `stickers`, `images` and
`audio` folders), then Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch > main / root.
